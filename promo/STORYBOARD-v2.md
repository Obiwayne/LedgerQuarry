# LedgerQuarry promo v2: storyboard ("Apple-style" cut)

20.0 s, 1080 × 1920, 60 fps = 1200 frames. Snappier than v1: a beat every 1.5–2 s, mostly type, light middle section.
Reference studied: @thednyx "whop saas explainer" (TikTok, 21 s): dark kinetic opener → one-word punch → cut to a bright Apple-style
background → notification card → icons inside the type → product cards with a cursor click → big logo + "Sell anything." → dark end card.
Our own icons and copy throughout; nothing copied from the reference.

Palette: dark ink #0b0d12 for the opener and end card; light #f4f5f7 with soft mint and lilac glows for the middle; text #0f1115 on light;
mint #3ddc97 accent; app UI stays dark (dark phone on a light stage).
Audio: female voiceover (Kokoro), faster jazz bed (126 bpm, snaps on 2 and 4), whoosh on each cut, pop on each card, click on the tap.
