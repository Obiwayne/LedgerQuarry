# LedgerQuarry promo: storyboard

20.0 s, 1080 × 1920 (9:16 for TikTok, Reels, Shorts, X), 60 fps = 1200 frames.
Path: hook → problem → product fixes it → payoff.
Brand: ink #0b0d12, mint #3ddc97, orange block #fd8b40, Space Grotesk headlines, Inter body.
Audio: female voiceover (Kokoro, local), original jazz bed (Rhodes, upright bass, brushes) ducked under the voice.
